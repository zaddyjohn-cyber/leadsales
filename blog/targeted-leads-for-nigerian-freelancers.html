The math is clear. You need targeted leads.

What Are Targeted Business Leads?

Targeted business leads are company contact details (name, email, phone, website) filtered by specific criteria that matter to your service. Instead of getting 10 million random emails, you get 500 real businesses in Lagos that actually need what you sell.

The Core Elements You Get

  • Business name and industry — so you know exactly who they are
  • Decision-maker email — not the generic info@company.com, but the owner or manager who can actually hire you
  • Phone number — for follow-up if email doesn't convert
  • Website — to research the business and personalize your pitch
  • Filter options — by city, industry, company size, revenue range

This means zero cold-calling random numbers, zero mass-blasting generic emails, and zero hoping someone responds.

How to Pitch Targeted Leads (The Cold Email Formula)

Having leads is 20% of the equation. Pitching them well is 80%.

Step 1: Research the Business (5 Minutes)

Before you write anything, visit their website and understand:

  • What they sell or do
  • Who their target customer is
  • What problems they likely face (slow website? no social media? bad copywriting?)

Step 2: Write a Short, Specific Email (3–4 Sentences)

Formula:

  1. Opener: Reference something specific about their business (not "Hi there")
  2. Problem: Name the specific problem they face
  3. Solution: What you do and the outcome
  4. CTA: Single, easy ask (book a call or reply with feedback)

Example (for a web designer):

Hi Chioma,

I noticed your e-commerce store on Shopify — the product pages are clean, but I see you're losing potential sales because customers can't filter by size or colour easily. I help store owners add smart product filters that increase average order value by 20–35%.

Are you open to a 15-minute call this week to see if this applies to your business?

Best,
[Your name]

This is personalized, specific, and focuses on their problem — not your credentials.

Step 4: Follow Up (Most Responses Come from Follow-Ups)

80% of sales happen after the 5th touch point. Your first email might get ignored — that's normal. Send 2–3 follow-ups over 2 weeks:

Payment Methods for Nigerian Freelancers

When you close a deal via cold email, how do you get paid?

  • Payoneer — Most Nigerian businesses know this; they can pay you directly, and you transfer to Zenith, GTBank, or other Nigerian banks
  • Wise (formerly TransferWise) — Lowest fees for international payments; great if the client pays from abroad
  • Direct bank transfer — Local clients will often pay directly to your Nigerian account
  • Grey (formerly Grey App) — Growing option for direct USD receipts

Pro tip: When quoting, ask if they prefer naira or USD. If they have USD, price in dollars — you'll earn more and avoid NEPA blackouts eating into your profit.

Common Mistakes Nigerian Freelancers Make with Cold Email

Mistake 1: Being Too Salesy

"Hi, I'm the BEST web designer in Nigeria! Let me build your site for only ₦50,000!"

Wrong. This reads as desperate. Instead: "I noticed your site loads slow on mobile — that costs you about 30% of potential customers."

Mistake 2: Not Following Up

You send one email and never hear back. That's normal. Most people don't respond to the first email. Send follow-ups. Use Zaram Web Mailer to automate this so you're not manually tracking 50 emails.

Mistake 3: Pitching Everyone the Same Way

If you're pitching an e-commerce store, a law firm, and a restaurant with identical emails, your response rate tanks. Spend 5 minutes personalizing each pitch — mention something specific about their business.

Mistake 4: Targeting the Wrong People

You need decision-makers' emails, not generic info@ addresses. This is why Freelance LeadsHub verified leads matter — they give you actual manager and owner emails, not dead ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many targeted leads should I buy to get my first client?

A: Start with 100–200 leads and send personalized pitches to all of them. With a 5–10% response rate, you'll get 5–20 conversations. Close 1–2 of those. Once you see what works, you can confidently scale to 500+ leads. Don't buy 5,000 leads as a beginner — test first, scale later.

Q: What's the difference between targeted leads and spam email lists?

A: Spam lists are old, unverified, and filled with dead emails. Targeted leads are recent, verified, and filtered by specific criteria you choose. A spam list has a 0.5% response rate; targeted leads give you 5–15%. You pay for quality — ₦20,000 for 500 good leads beats ₦2,000 for 50,000 garbage emails.

Q: How long does it take to see results from cold email?

A: First responses usually come within 24–48 hours. Most campaigns take 2–4 weeks to see meaningful traction. If you send 50 emails on Monday, by Friday you might have 3–5 interested prospects. Give it 30 days before deciding if a campaign worked or not.

Q: Can I use cold email if I'm just starting out as a freelancer?

A: Yes, but you need a portfolio or case study first. If you have zero work samples, build 2–3 pieces for free or at low cost, then start cold emailing. Businesses won't hire based on credentials alone — they want to see your work. Once you have samples, cold email becomes powerful.
