Method 1: Google Search Operators (Free, Time-Intensive)

This is the foundation. If you only have ₦0 to spend, start here.

The basic formula:

  1. Open Google
  2. Search: site:businessname.com.ng "email" OR "contact us"
  3. Repeat for 20–50 businesses manually

This works, but it's slow. For a targeted list of 100 business emails in your niche, you're looking at 6–10 hours of manual work. At ₦3,000/hour (a modest freelancer rate), that's ₦18,000–₦30,000 in time cost before you even send your first email.

When to use it: If you want to test a niche with zero budget, or if you're targeting fewer than 20 specific businesses.

Method 2: LinkedIn Sales Navigator (Paid, Medium Effort)

LinkedIn is packed with Nigerian business decision-makers. Sales Navigator costs about $45/month (roughly ₦27,000 at current exchange rates), and you can filter by location, industry, job title, and company size.

How it works:

  1. Search for your ideal customer (e.g., "E-commerce store owners in Lagos")
  2. Visit their profiles and look for email addresses in the "About" section or contact info
  3. Use Hunter.io or RocketReach to verify/find emails for companies
  4. Export and organize into a spreadsheet

Realistically, you'll find 30–50 verified emails per week with this method. That's 120–200 emails/month, which is solid for ongoing outreach.

Cost: ₦27,000/month (LinkedIn) + ₦3,000–₦5,000/month (email verification tool)

When to use it: If you have ₦30,000/month in your freelance budget and want consistent, quality leads over time.

Method 3: Nigerian Business Directories & Websites

Nigeria has several business listing sites. These are goldmines if you know where to look.

Key directories:

  • Naijaads.com – Local classified ads with business contact details
  • Naijapages.com.ng – Business listings by category
  • BusinessDirectory.ng – Searchable database of Nigerian companies
  • TradeKey – Global + Nigerian B2B marketplace
  • Alibaba (Seller Database) – Find Nigerian exporters and manufacturers
  • Chamber of Commerce websites – Lagos Chamber, Kaduna Chamber, etc.

You can manually scrape these, or hire a data entry person on Fiverr for ₦2,000–₦5,000 to extract 100 emails and phone numbers in 2–3 days.

Cost: ₦0–₦5,000 (if hiring help)

Time per 100 emails: 8–10 hours (DIY) or 2–3 days (outsourced)

When to use it: When you're targeting a specific niche (e.g., manufacturers, exporters, e-commerce stores) and want to control the data quality yourself.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. Sending too many emails at once
If you send 5,000 emails in one day from a new account, you'll trigger spam filters. Space them over 1–2 weeks. Zaram Web Mailer handles this automatically.

2. Using a generic pitch**
"Hello, I'm a freelancer. Hire me." → 0% response rate. Personalize. Reference their business, website, or recent social media post.

3. Not following up**
Most people need 3–5 touches before they respond. Don't give up after one email.

4. Wrong email addresses**
If you're manually scraping emails, verify them first. Use Hunter.io or RocketReach. A list with 30% invalid emails wastes your time and money.

5. Competing on price**
When you reach out directly, never lead with "I'm cheaper than Fiverr." Lead with results: "I designed 47 Shopify stores that averaged ₦2.3M in first-month sales."

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is it legal to cold email businesses in Nigeria?

A: Yes, cold emailing is legal in Nigeria. However, follow basic etiquette: use a professional email address, clearly state who you are, and include an unsubscribe option in your email footer. Don't send to personal emails or spam anyone. Treat your cold email list as a business resource, not a spam tool. Nigerian businesses receive cold emails daily and expect them as part of B2B communication.

Q: How many cold emails should I send per day?

A: Start with 50–100 emails per day for the first week to test your templates and warm up your email account. After 1 week, if you're not hitting spam filters, increase to 200–300/day. Never send more than 500 in one day from a single account—this triggers Gmail and Outlook's spam detection. Use Zaram Web Mailer to space them out automatically. See our full article on how many cold emails to send per day for detailed guidelines.

Q: What's the difference between cold email and cold WhatsApp/DM?

A: Email gets a 15–25% open rate with proper templates. WhatsApp gets 70%+ open rate but can feel intrusive. Cold DMs on Instagram/LinkedIn get 5–10% response rates. Best strategy: use email first (scalable, professional), then follow up on WhatsApp if you get engagement. Read our detailed comparison: cold DM vs. cold email for Nigerian freelancers.

Q: Can I still use Upwork and Fiverr while doing cold email outreach?

A: Absolutely. Cold email is for scaling beyond ₦200,000/month. Use Fiverr/Upwork as a secondary revenue stream while building your direct client base. However, many successful Nigerian freelancers eventually move 80–90% of their income to direct clients because the rates are 3–5x higher and you keep 100% of the money (no platform commission).
