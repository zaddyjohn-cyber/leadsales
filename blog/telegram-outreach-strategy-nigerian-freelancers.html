Why Telegram Works Better Than Upwork for Nigerian Freelancers

Let's be honest: Upwork and Fiverr are broken for most Nigerian freelancers.

You spend 10+ hours per week writing proposals. You're competing against 200+ other bidders. Clients negotiate your price down 40%. Platform fees strip ₦15,000 from a ₦75,000 project before you withdraw to Payoneer (and Payoneer takes another cut).

Telegram is different because:

  • Direct access to decision-makers. You're messaging the business owner or manager directly, not fighting an algorithm.
  • Telegram is where Nigerian business owners already are. Unlike LinkedIn, which many Nigerian SMEs don't actively use, Telegram is mainstream for networking and business communication.
  • Higher perceived value. A personalized Telegram message from a freelancer with a portfolio feels more serious than a generic Upwork proposal.
  • No platform fees. 100% of what you earn goes to you (minus payment processor fees like Wise or Payoneer, which are much lower than Upwork's 5%).
  • Faster sales cycles. Direct communication means negotiations happen in real-time, not over days.
  • You can build actual relationships. Telegram becomes your channel to nurture repeat business and referrals.

Real talk: a freelancer using Telegram outreach can close 2–4 clients per month earning ₦200,000–₦500,000 ($130–$330 USD at current rates) each, compared to 1–2 clients on Upwork earning less and paying 5% in fees. The math favors Telegram.

Step 2: Set Up Your Telegram Profile Like a Business (Not a Random Person)

Your Telegram profile is your first impression. Make it count.

Profile picture: Use a professional headshot, not a meme or blurry photo. This signals you're serious.

Bio: Write something like: "Web Designer | E-commerce Sites | Portfolio: [link] | Available for Projects" or "Content Writer | Blog Posts | Email Sequences | DM for rates"

Bio link: Link to a simple portfolio or landing page. If you don't have one, create a free Google Site or Carrd page (carrd.co takes 15 minutes). Make sure it shows your best 3–5 past projects and your rate range in USD.

Status: Update it every week. Example: "Booking web design clients — 2-week turnaround" or "Open for content projects."

The goal: when someone clicks your profile after you message them, they see a professional offering a specific service—not a personal account.

Step 3: Write Your Telegram Outreach Message (The Template That Works)

This is critical. Most Nigerian freelancers send generic spam messages. That gets ignored or blocked.

Here's the template that actually converts:

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company Name] is in [industry]. I help businesses like yours with [specific service—web design, content, social media management].

I recently worked on a [similar project type] for [client type], which resulted in [specific result—increased conversions by 40%, reduced bounce rate, etc.].

Are you open to exploring how I can help [specific business outcome—drive more traffic, improve rankings, etc.]?

Here's my portfolio: [link]. Happy to discuss rates and timeline.

Cheers,
[Your name]

Why this works:

  • Personalized. You mention their company name (from your lead list).
  • Specific. You're not offering "services"—you're offering web design for e-commerce or content for SaaS.
  • Proof. You mention a result (even if it's approximate or from a similar client).
  • Clear ask. You're asking for a conversation, not a sale.
  • Short. 5–6 lines max. Busy business owners read it in 30 seconds.

Pro tip: Don't mention price in the first message. Let them ask.

Avoiding Common Mistakes

Mistake 1: Sending Generic Messages

"I offer freelance services. Interested?" gets deleted instantly. Use the template above instead.

Mistake 2: Following Up Too Soon

Don't send a follow-up message after 1 day. Wait 3–5 days, then send a different message: "Hey [name], checking in on my previous message. Still happy to discuss how I can help with [specific service]."

Mistake 3: Not Having a Portfolio or Rate Card

When someone asks "What are your rates?" and you don't have a clear answer, you lose the deal. Have a rate card ready. Example: "Web design: ₦150,000–₦400,000 depending on pages and features. Content writing: ₦5,000–₦15,000 per article."

Mistake 4: Mixing Personal and Professional

Don't post memes and party photos on your business Telegram profile. Keep it professional. Use a separate personal account for friends.

Mistake 5: Not Following Up on Responses

Someone replies "Tell me more" and you disappear for 3 days. Reply within 2 hours. Speed wins deals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Will Telegram outreach get my account banned?

A: No, if you follow Telegram's terms. You're sending direct messages to business owners—that's not spam. Telegram bans accounts that send unsolicited messages to thousands of random users or use bots to scrape data. If you're sending 15–20 personalized messages per day to real businesses and keeping your account in good standing, you're fine. Use Zaram Web Mailer to avoid rate-limiting issues by spacing out sends over time.

Q: How do I know if the phone numbers in Freelance LeadsHub are real and current?

A: The leads are sourced from public business registries, Google Business listings, and industry databases. Most numbers are current, but expect 5–15% to be outdated or inactive. That's normal in any lead list. The response rate of 3–7% factors this in. When someone doesn't respond after 2 follow-ups, move on—that's a bad number or someone not interested.

Q: What if a client asks for my rate before I've shown my portfolio?

A: Ask them about their project first. Say: "Happy to help! Tell me more about what you need—timeline, scope, specific deliverables. Once I understand the full project, I can give you an accurate quote." This protects you from lowball offers and shows professionalism. If they insist on a rate before discussing the project, they're likely a price-shopper. Move on to the next lead.

Q: Can I use the same message for all 8,500 leads, or do I need to customize each one?

A: Customize the opening. Use the template but change [Company Name] and [Name] for each message—that takes 2 seconds per lead with Zaram's merge tag feature. The body can stay 80% the same. Personalization in the greeting is what matters for high response rates.
